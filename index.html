How to use BlinkPuppet

  1. Click Start camera and allow access when your browser asks.
  2. Pick a character and look straight ahead, then press Recenter.
  3. For streaming, choose Green screen, click Clean view, and capture this browser window in OBS with a Chroma Key filter. Press Esc to leave Clean view.

Frequently asked questions

Is BlinkPuppet free?

Yes. It is free to use, with no login, no download and no account.

Is my webcam video uploaded anywhere?

No. Face tracking runs entirely in your browser. No video or image is sent to a server or stored.

How do I use the avatar in OBS?

Open BlinkPuppet in Chrome or Edge, choose the green background, click Clean view, then add a Window Capture in OBS and apply a Chroma Key filter.

Can I use it as a virtual camera in Zoom or Google Meet?

Not directly, because websites cannot create a virtual camera. Share the browser window in your call, or capture it in OBS and start the OBS Virtual Camera.

Why is my avatar choppy or delayed?

Close other heavy tabs, use Chrome or Edge, and make sure your room is well lit. Older phones and laptops may run slower.
